# Coffee Shop Simple Sales Analysis 
Analyzing coffee shop sales performance.

## Business Questions
1. What is the total revenue?
2. Which product generates the most revenue?
3. Which day is the slowest? 
4. What payment method is most used?
5. What are the peak hours?

## Files
- `coffee_schema.sql` - table structure
- `coffee_data.csv` - sample 10 rows 
- `coffee_queries.sql` - 5 business queries with explanations
- `cofee_power_bi` - visualization of the result

## Key Insight 
- Total revenue for the day comes mostly from coffee sales.
- Latte generates the highest revenue.
- Tuesday is the slowest day.
- Cash is most used, followed by GCash and card.
- Morning is the strongest, peaking at 8 AM then 9 AM.
#
Recommendation:
- Run Tuesday promos to fix slow day.
- Offer espresso as add-on or bundle with pastry to boost sales.
- Add afternoon happy hour (2-4 PM) to fix slow hour.
